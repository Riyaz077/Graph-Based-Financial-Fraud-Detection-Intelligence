# Model Card — GraphShield Demo

- **Purpose:** educational exploration of graph-informed transaction classification.
- **Model:** compact GraphSAGE-style message-passing classifier; Random Forest baseline.
- **Input:** transaction attributes and sender/receiver account activity/network features.
- **Training data:** synthetic transactions generated by `src/data.py`.
- **Known limitations:** synthetic labels are generated from a simple rule; no temporal graph learning, identity resolution, streaming ingestion, calibrated thresholds, or production monitoring is implemented.
- **Risks:** false positives may inconvenience legitimate customers; false negatives may miss suspicious activity. Risk scores can encode bias if trained on unrepresentative data.
- **Appropriate use:** portfolio demonstration, classroom learning and controlled experimentation only.
- **Inappropriate use:** live payment blocking, customer accusations, credit decisions, law-enforcement action or regulatory compliance claims.
